## Overview

A metronome that can reproduce musical scores!

### Basic Metronome
Designed with an emphasis on quick operation. In addition to a wide variety of tempo adjustment methods, you can quickly fine-tune values by swiping vertically (or using mouse scroll) without having to tap repeatedly.

### Custom Sounds
In addition to the 5 basic built-in sounds, you can register up to 5 of your own custom sound patterns.
You can also download various sample sounds from the [in-app link](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1uftx_yTjGCWsNSE_bHz-2QX7BZl-wYjD?usp=drive_link) (Google Drive) and register them in your custom slots.

### Song Mode - A great ally for ensemble practice!
You can create song data to play the metronome along with.
Based on the idea of looking like a musical score without needing to input actual notes, measures are represented by multi-measure rests.
In NamaMetro, a section represented by a multi-measure rest is called a "measure group" (小節群).
I've included as many flexible settings as I could think of for measure groups: rit. and accel., off-beat ticks, complex time signatures, individual playback editing, repeat signs, copy/paste/delete from multiple selections, and more.

### Playback Mode
The "Playback Mode", which pairs with the song data creation, also pursues comfort.
Count-in before starting the performance. The ability to start from anywhere, whether it's the second time through a repeat or the 4th measure of a rehearsal mark. A "Multiplier" feature to practice at different playback speeds (speed can be adjusted by vertical swiping!). A read-aloud function so you don't get lost (counting beats, rehearsal mark names, etc.).

### Perfect Backup
All settings can be exported as files.
In addition to a full export that outputs everything together, there is also a function to output sounds and songs one by one, allowing you to share data with your band members.

### App Support (PWA)
Although it is an app that runs on a browser, it has a function to install it on various OSs. (Details are described at the bottom of the "Settings" tab in the app.)
Once installed, you can place it on your home screen or desktop, use it offline, and it will automatically update to the latest stable version when reloaded while connected to the internet.

### Screen Guide
1) Metronome: Swipe numbers vertically (or scroll) to change tempo fast; the tap tempo and ± buttons work too. Beat patterns set each beat to strong / medium / weak / off, and presets hold 10 slots.
2) Settings: Register up to 5 of your own sound files on top of the 5 built-in tones. Themes, flash / vibration, voice & external-device control, and data import / export live here.
3) Song Mode (Edit): Arrange measure groups to build a whole piece - measures, tempo and time signature per group, with rit. / accel. and repeat signs.
4) Song Mode (Play): Start from any measure group or measure, with count-in. The multiplier changes playback speed for practice, and read-aloud keeps your place.

### Extras
You can also feel the beat through screen flashes and vibration, and start/stop playback by voice or an external device. Sleep prevention, theme colors, and exporting song data as MP3 round out a toolkit full of small touches that help in real practice sessions.
